🎉 Welcome to the Official Release of Relay V1.0! 🎉

We are thrilled to finally launch Relay, your ultimate messaging, community, and entertainment hub! Here are the incredible features waiting for you:

🚀 1. Lightning Fast Messaging: Send text, voice notes, photos, and videos instantly. React, reply, or even edit your messages within 15 minutes!

👻 2. Ultimate Privacy: Enable Disappearing Messages to make chats self-destruct. Send 'View Once' media for maximum security. Plus, full control over who sees your Last Seen, Read Receipts, and Profile Picture!

🌟 4. Moments (Stories): Share your life updates with 24-hour disappearing Moments! Post photos and text, and easily see who viewed your stories.

🤖 5. Meet Your New Best Friends - Mica & Mars: Play games, generate images, and chat with our advanced AI! Type !scramble to start a fast-paced word guessing game with your friends, and track your rank on the Global Leaderboard! Admins can swap between Mica's friendliness and Mars' sarcasm by typing !swap.

👤 6. Multi-Account Manager: Got an alter ego? Add multiple accounts and switch between them instantly without ever logging out.

🔔 7. Smart Background Notifications: Never miss a beat. Reply to messages and mark them as read directly from your phone's notification panel, even when the app is completely closed.

Relay is built for speed, privacy, and fun. Thank you for being a part of our journey from day one. Enjoy the app, explore the features, and let's get chatting! 🚀💖
